IME Studio — for Dr. Tess

Draft thorough, evidence-supported IME reports — faster.

Paste the case packet (records summary, history, physical exam, imaging). The studio drafts a complete Independent Medical Examination report with a Missouri-statute PPD percentage at the correct schedule level under RSMo § 287.190, the “prevailing factor” causation analysis under § 287.020.3, MMI status, work restrictions, and future-care recommendations. Every output is a draft for Dr. Tess’s review and signature.

The AI backend isn’t configured yet. Add an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY to your .env file and restart the server to enable drafting.
Clinical-use disclaimer. This tool produces a working draft from the material you paste. It does not access outside records. It does not assign a final PPD percentage on its own — Dr. Tess must verify every finding, confirm the statutory schedule level, adjust percentages based on her examination, and sign the final report. The draft can only be as accurate, complete, and impartial as the source material you provide. An IME must remain independent: include all findings the records support, not findings they do not.
Scan or upload medical records
Drop PDFs or scanned images (PNG / JPG) here. The studio reads every page and fills the form fields below — you review and edit before drafting. No limit on the number of files; large packets are automatically batched. (Note: the Anthropic API caps a single PDF at 32 MB / 100 pages — if you have a larger file, split it into multiple PDFs, e.g. one per provider.)

    The drafter uses these statutory weeks (RSMo § 287.190.1) when locating a scheduled rating. Dr. Tess should confirm the level on every case.

            Statutory references: causation “prevailing factor” standard — RSMo § 287.020.3;
