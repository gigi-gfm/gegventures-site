IME Studio — for Dr. Garcia

Draft Garcia Family Medicine IME letters — in her format.

Upload the records, enter today’s exam, and the studio drafts a full IME letter in Dr. Garcia’s established format — letterhead, addressee, RE block, Description of Injury, Present Symptoms, history sections, Physical Exam, numbered narrative analysis of every records source, and the Discussion section in question-and-answer form. Missouri “prevailing factor” causation, MMI determination, PPD percentage at the correct § 287.190 statutory level (or explicit deferral if not at MMI), and future-care recommendations under § 287.140. Signed Theresa C. Garcia MD, FAAFP, Dipl. ABOM. Every output is a draft for her review.

The AI backend isn’t configured yet. Add an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY to your .env file and restart the server to enable drafting.
Clinical-use disclaimer. This tool produces a working draft from the material you paste. It does not access outside records. It does not assign a final PPD percentage on its own — Dr. Tess must verify every finding, confirm the statutory schedule level, adjust percentages based on her examination, and sign the final report. The draft can only be as accurate, complete, and impartial as the source material you provide. An IME must remain independent: include all findings the records support, not findings they do not.
