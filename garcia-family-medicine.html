This is a working example of what Gegventures builds for healthcare practices: an AI assistant that greets website visitors, answers their questions, and turns interest into booked appointments — around the clock.

How it generates patients

From first question to booked appointment

Most prospective patients arrive with questions before they ever pick up the phone. The assistant meets them in that moment — answering clearly, building trust, and guiding them to book.

  • Answers questions about services, hours, insurance and location
  • Collects name, contact and reason for visit when a visitor is ready
  • Stays on-script — no medical advice, no invented details
  • HIPAA-conscious: only the minimum information is ever requested
  • Routes urgent situations to call 911 immediately
